# Release notes

## 4.1.0 — 2026-10-07

- Offline spelling and common English grammar underlines, explicit correction suggestions, and a personal dictionary.
- Poetry & prose tools with a bundled pronunciation dictionary, syllable counts, stress hints, rhythm and rhyme colors, and rhyme lookup with a syllable filter.
- Personal reading marks and pauses, rhyme maps with your own labels, repetition and motif exploration, fixed reference cards, private pass notes, and section bookmarks.
- Formatted HTML, PDF, DOCX and ODT exports; desktop handoffs to Writer, PDF viewers, Files, and mail composition; separate imports of revised office copies.
- Desktop file opening that preserves existing drafts, plus article and linked evidence handoff to Open Pressroom.
- Debian/Ubuntu installer with offline spelling dependencies, bundled dictionary licensing, PEP 517 packaging, regression checks, and a release checksum.

Proofreading and craft tools run locally without AI generation. Craft annotations remain separate from manuscript saves, exports, and publishing handoffs. English pronunciation and stress displays are hints; your own reading can differ.

The installer requires Python 3.9+, GTK 4.10+, and libadwaita 1.4+. Ollama remains optional. See [README.md](README.md#installation) for installation, upgrades, and moving from a source install.
